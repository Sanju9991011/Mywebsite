About Us

Fresh Flower Shop was established in 2020 with a mission to bring happiness through flowers. We specialize in delivering fresh, hand-picked flowers across Sri Lanka. Our skilled florists create beautiful bouquets for birthdays, weddings, and all special occasions.

We believe that flowers can express emotions better than words, and our goal is to make every delivery memorable. Visit our store or order online for a delightful floral experience!
